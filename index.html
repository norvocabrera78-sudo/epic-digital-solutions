Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after ownership is transferred to me?

Once your project is completed and deployed, we transfer 100% full administrative rights and control over to you. You own your site and automations outright—no forced long-term lock-ins or hidden fees.

Do I need to buy my own domain and hosting?

To ensure you retain complete ownership, we will guide you through setting up your own affordable domain and hosting accounts, or we can deploy directly onto your existing provider.

Can I make minor text or image updates myself?

Absolutely. We build using clean, accessible structures so you can easily handle small changes. However, if you prefer a complete hands-off approach, our Premium Membership or On-Demand plans are always available.

How do the 3 monthly modifications work in the Premium Membership?

A modification includes standard requests like swapping out images or flyers in a gallery, editing text announcements, changing pricing details, or tweaking layout features. Major redesigns or completely new feature builds are quoted separately.
