Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after ownership is transferred to me?

Once your project is completed and deployed, we transfer 100% full administrative rights and control to you. You can make any changes you like, anytime. We don't retain any access or control.

Do I need to buy my own domain and hosting?

To ensure you retain complete ownership, we will guide you through setting up your own affordable domain name and hosting account. You stay in control, and we assist with the technical setup.

Can I make minor text or image updates myself?

Absolutely. We build using clean, accessible structures so you can easily handle small changes. However, if you'd prefer hands-off management, our support plans can handle updates for you.

How do the 3 monthly modifications work in the Premium Membership?

A modification includes standard requests like swapping out images or flyers in a gallery, editing text, updating pricing or dates, layout tweaks, or adding new features. Think of it as access to our team for routine updates.
